The Confront

The Confront is an accessible 3D audio shooter powered by King Audio Engine. Explore maps, fight enemies, earn experience, level up, and resurrect to keep advancing.

Objective

Survive. Defeat the enemies you encounter, avoid being defeated, and strengthen your character. Battles grant experience and can reward currency and other items.

What you will find in the game

  • 3D maps with distinct environments, native King Audio reverberation zones, and challenges.
  • Playable characters (Stefan and Annie) with unique stats and audio profiles.
  • Enemies across classes including animals, robots, zombies, spirits, and bosses.
  • Weapons requiring cardinal alignment, ammunition, equipment, medical items, packages, and in-map stores.
  • Experience, levels, and resurrections that scale progression and unlock new challenges.
  • Travel points, elevators, and interactive doors with room acoustics.

Maps can be unlocked as you progress and may also be restricted based on level or resurrection requirements shown in-game.

Default Controls

Elevators: when at an elevator door, press Alt+Enter to enter the cabin. Inside, press Enter to start the ride automatically when there is one destination or open the floor selector; on arrival, press Enter to open the doors and exit. Outside an elevator, Alt+Enter interacts with physical doors or uses a valid TravelPoint.

  • Arrow Keys: move forward, backward, left, and right.
  • Q and E: turn left and right.
  • Spacebar: jump.
  • Left Ctrl: fire the equipped weapon.
  • R: reload; A: check ammo status.
  • Enter: use the selected item from the inventory.
  • Shift+Enter: interact with an in-map store.
  • Alt+Enter: open or close a door, use an elevator, or activate a TravelPoint.
  • Tab: switch inventory categories (All, Weapons & Ammo, Equipment, Medical, Currencies).
  • C: check coordinates (Shift+C for equipped gear); Z: check location/zone.
  • N: track nearby NPCs (including current health, distance, and direction).
  • H: check health; L: check level and progress; K: check kills.
  • B: open builder menu (when available); Esc: exit.

Controls can be customized in the game settings. The list above describes the default bindings.
