Version 1.0.0 - The Confrontation
=================================

NAME AND VERSION

CHANGED: The game is now called The Confrontation and its version is 1.0.0.
CHANGED: The new name and version are shown in the title bar, in every window title and in settings/versions.cfg.
CHANGED: Every header comment, comment, default text and settings file that still carried the old name was updated.

REMOVED SYSTEMS

REMOVED: The complete NPC system. The NPC runtime, the NPC class and its state, the NPC spawning and spawning points, the NPC management menu and builder tools, the NPC navigation, the NPC combat and decision code, the NPC voices in the sound pools, the NPC entries in maps and stores, and every helper that only existed to serve an NPC were deleted.
REMOVED: Hitting, targeting, damaging and killing NPCs. Projectiles now only collide with the world geometry, walls, map bounds and their own range; there is no target search, no hit registration on characters and no damage dealt to anything that the NPC system used to provide.
CHANGED: The player progression that lived inside the NPC runtime (experience, level and resurrection handling) was moved out into its own player module, so the player systems keep working without the NPC runtime.

REMOVED: The complete hands system. The left hand and the right hand slots, the second hand, the hand switching, the per-hand weapon state, the per-hand drawing, firing and reloading rules, the dual wielding and every message, sound and check that referred to a hand were deleted.
CHANGED: The player now has one equipped weapon at a time. Equipping a weapon replaces the previous one, and firing, reloading, ammunition and the walk speed always read the single equipped weapon.

REMOVED: The complete day and night system. The game calendar, the world clock, sunrise and sunset, dawn and dusk twilight, noon, midnight, the day phases, the seasons, the leap years, the weekdays, the holidays, the solstices and equinoxes and the moon phases are all gone. Nothing in the game measures or advances time any more.
REMOVED: Every spoken time and date announcement: sunrise, morning, noon, afternoon, sunset, twilight, night, midnight, the year change, the holiday and solstice messages, the upcoming holiday warnings and the full moon announcements.
REMOVED: The world clock persistence, including its own save file settings/game_time.dat. Saving, dying, leaving the game, changing maps and starting a new game no longer write or read any calendar state, because there is no calendar state. The old save file was deleted as well.
REMOVED: The Shift+T shortcut that spoke the current game time, date, season, day phase, moon phase, solstice and holiday.
REMOVED: The day and night time limits of map sound sources and map ambience: the "Use day and night time limits" checkbox, the start hour, start minute, end hour, end minute and fade duration sliders, the time window stored in the map lines and the cross-fades that followed the time of day. A sound source now simply plays at the volume of its volume slider, and an ambience simply plays while the player is inside its area.
REMOVED: The calendar object that could be placed on maps: its builder entry, its builder form, its runtime class, its spawn and check functions, its press and loop sounds, its map line and its access to the game date, season, holiday and moon phase.
REMOVED: The clock object that could be placed on maps: its builder entry, its builder form, its runtime class, its spawn and check functions, its press and loop sounds, its map line and its access to the game time.
REMOVED: The "calendar" and "clock" entries from the construction menu, together with their sound folder translations and the fallback sound names that belonged to them.
REMOVED: The map content that was built on the removed system: the night ambience of the main map, the day and night music pairs of the main map and of the north ocean, and the day and night ocean sources of the north ocean. Every map keeps one ambience and one source per area that is always available.
REMOVED: The entire collection of time driven logic that the removal left behind, including the unused helpers for time windows, the unused decibel conversion helper and the dead branches that called them.

REMOVED: The message of the day system in full. The daily message pool, its file settings/daily_messages.txt, its state file settings/daily_msg_state.dat, the pool loader, the sequential rotation, the index persistence, the daily announcement at startup, the F1 announcement and the "daily message manager" entry in the construction menu were deleted.
CHANGED: Only the rolling announcements remain. They are read from settings/broadcasts.txt and one random message is spoken every 10 to 15 minutes, without any reference to a day, a date or a world clock.

SOUND

CHANGED: The sound pool was updated to the current King Audio components and now runs on the king_audio_engine version of the pool. All spatial positioning goes through King Audio's own set_position(), including its HRTF handling.
CHANGED: The spatial behaviour was rebuilt for King Audio: box clamping, distance gating, occlusion raycasts, position smoothing and the per-frame listener update were adapted to the engine instead of the old mixer.
KEPT: The project customizations on top of the current King Audio set: low-pass filter command support, offset tracking and seeking in every play mode, start pitch applied after play() because King Audio ignores it before a channel exists, and a single king_system_update() per frame committed by the caller instead of once per pool.
FIXED: Footsteps keep a constant volume of 0 dB and vary only in pitch, so walking no longer gets louder or quieter at random.
FIXED: The sound pool no longer leaks slots or voices when a sound ends, is stopped or is replaced.
FIXED: The local sounds of enemies and allies (footsteps, weapon draw, reload, shield equip, healing and the near crack of a shot) now have their own pool with a radius of a dozen steps. A fight far away is no longer heard as if it were next to the player.
CHANGED: An exchange of fire is heard as exactly one report per shot: up to ten steps the player hears the real shot of the weapon, and from ten steps on only the weapon's own distant sample, which is a different recording. The near crack and the distant report no longer play at the same time and the near one never travels.
FIXED: The distant report used to fall back to the real shot of the weapon when no distant sample was configured, and that is what made a far exchange sound like the near crack crossing the map. The fallback is gone: without a distant sample the shot is simply not heard from afar.
FIXED: The distant report level now follows the model of War Cry, which was the reference for this behaviour: the shot is played at full level from the near radius on and loses one tenth of a decibel per step, so 10 dB at 100 steps, 20 at 200 and 30 at 300, whatever the size of the map. The engine falloff is no longer involved: before, King Audio silenced the report at 500 units and the curve also dropped 12 dB by 40 steps, which is why an exchange a block away was barely there.
FIXED: King Audio reaches silence at 125 / volume_step and the distant pool was set to 0.25, which is only 500 units, while the +6 dB gain it asked for was discarded because a sound volume cannot go above 0 dB. Far reports were therefore inaudible on any map bigger than a few hundred steps. The pool no longer attenuates by itself and the computed report level does all of it.
ADDED: A report that is still sounding follows the character that fired it: its position moves with the shooter and its level re-reads the distance to the player every frame, so walking towards or away from an exchange raises and lowers it instead of leaving it stuck where the first shot happened. Sustained fire reuses the running report rather than stacking a copy per round.
KNOWN: Seven firearms carry no distant sample of their own (the auto pistol, the pistol, the auto rifle, the Awds SMG Uzi, the M17 Viper Uzi, the laser rifle and the crossbow), so they are not heard past ten steps until a sound_distant_fire is set in their .wpm file. Melee weapons are in the same situation by nature. The other 65 firearms already point at their own distant recordings.
REMOVED: The sleep system leftovers, including the fade and lowpass handling that only existed for it. Lowpass now belongs only to diving under water, and the fade that remains is the one used when the world is reset.

INVENTORY

REMOVED: The complete inventory menu. The full inventory window, its list, its categories, its navigation, its item actions, its open and close state and its own key handling were deleted.
CHANGED: The quick inventory is the only inventory interface. Tab and Shift+Tab move the focus from item to item in a cycle and announce the item, its amount and the position in the list.
CHANGED: Enter uses the focused quick inventory item: a healing item is used only while health is below its maximum, a shield is equipped and a weapon is equipped.
CHANGED: Escape does nothing and announces nothing, because there is no open or closed state and no "quick inventory closed" message any more.
REMOVED: The unused inventory position variable and every leftover of the old full inventory mode.
FIXED: Using an item from the quick inventory keeps the focus usable when the item is consumed or the list becomes empty.

WEAPONS

CHANGED: Every weapon reads its cadence, weight, time between shots, range, spread, damage, fire mode and ammunition from its own .wpm file. There is no longer any weapon timing, weight, ammunition rule or fire mode rule written in the code.
CHANGED: A weapon now has a single fire mode and it is chosen when the weapon is created: either semi-automatic or full automatic. Automatic weapons fire while the trigger is held, semi-automatic weapons need one press per shot.
REMOVED: The burst, scattershot and focused modes, the mode lists that combined several of them, the mode switching key, the multiple-mode state, the round counter of burst fire and every branch, table, name and entry that referred to them, in the player weapons, in the weapon builder and in the enemies and allies.
REMOVED: The minimum and maximum shot times. A weapon has one fire time and it is the only cadence that exists, in the file, in the weapon form, in the serialized bundle and in the loaded weapon data. Nothing calculates or clamps a range of shot times any more.
CHANGED: The weapon builder offers the projectile as a navigable list of the kinds the game really launches: automatic, ballistic, bolt, ball, energy, plasma, missile, rocket, grenade, fire and drainage. The focused entry is the one saved and there is no check or uncheck action. The free text projectile field is gone.
CHANGED: The fire mode of a weapon is also a list with exactly two entries, semi-automatic and full automatic. The focused entry is the mode that is saved.
FIXED: The time between shots of every weapon now follows its .wpm file exactly, and the weapon's own fire interval is what the enemies and allies use as well.
FIXED: Weapon fire, reload, draw, empty and distant sounds are resolved from the weapon file and the sound folders, with no generic fallback sound.

BALANCE

CHANGED: Pistols fire every 200 to 400 milliseconds, deal 200 to 400 damage and reach 11 to 13 steps. Their weight stays at the light end of the ladder.
CHANGED: Revolvers share the pistol base, with a fire time of 200 to 400 milliseconds, but deal 300 to 800 damage and reach 15 to 20 steps.
CHANGED: Shotguns are slower and heavier: 600 to 1500 milliseconds between shots, 800 to 1800 damage, 13 to 16 steps of reach and a walk interval above every pistol.
CHANGED: Machine guns are the fastest weapons: 70 to 100 milliseconds between shots, 900 to 2000 damage, 18 to 22 steps of reach and a heavy walk interval. They are the only class that stays fully automatic.
CHANGED: Rifles sit between machine guns and snipers: 250 to 500 milliseconds, 400 to 1285 damage, 25 to 30 steps and a heavier walk interval than a pistol.
CHANGED: Sniper rifles fire every 1000 to 3000 milliseconds, deal 2500 to 4850 damage, reach 45 to 50 steps and are the heaviest firearms to carry.
CHANGED: The cannon deals 3000 to 5000 damage, holds three rounds, reaches 50 steps and fires every three seconds. The other launchers scale down from it: the crossbow fires every 1.2 seconds for 800 to 1500 damage, the flamethrowers every second for 800 to 1500 damage and a shorter reach.
CHANGED: Melee weapons are not firearms: a blade reaches 5 to 7 steps and deals 200 to 400 damage, with a swing time between 400 and 800 milliseconds and no weight penalty.
CHANGED: The general ladder is kept everywhere: the bigger the weapon, the more damage it deals, the farther it reaches and the heavier it is to carry.
CHECKED: All 80 weapon files were rewritten through one balance pass, so no weapon kept an old cadence, an old damage range or a combined mode.

PERFORMANCE

FIXED: Starting a game, loading a map and reloading the map after a construction change could stall for almost a minute. The enemy and ally spawn search tried 500 positions and, at each one, walked down every single height level of the map, and each of those lookups fell back to splitting every map line as text. The search now reads the native map directly, tries a bounded number of positions, and gives up for the whole map once a map proves to have no floor at all. The same map load now takes milliseconds.
FIXED: Every enemy and ally movement check and every patrol pick used the same text fallback, which is what made the game stutter while characters walked. They read the native map now.

CONTROLS

CHANGED: The Spacebar alone jumps again. It is the only jump key and it works on its own, without a modifier.
REMOVED: Ctrl + Space. Pressing Control and Space together no longer jumps, does not fire and performs no action at all.
CHANGED: Only the left Control key fires. The right Control key was removed from the firing key, from the default key binding and from every place that accepted either Control.
CHANGED: The default binding of the fire action is now the left Control key instead of the Spacebar, so the keyboard focus and the jump can never share a key.
REMOVED: The V key that switched the fire mode, because a weapon has a single mode.

CURRENCY AND STORES

REMOVED: The four coins that were hardcoded in the game. The four native currencies, gold, golden crystal, diamond and silver, are now ordinary item files and the store uses them like any other item.
REMOVED: The currency aliases and legacy names that were accepted in silence, and the hidden fallback that charged gold whenever a currency could not be found. A store now charges exactly the currency it declares.
FIXED: The four currencies are offered by the store as payment methods, can be spent, earned and checked, and appear in the store currency selection.
REMOVED: The stub that reported every store product as unavailable and the dead store code that surrounded it. A store product is now granted only when its item exists, with no silent substitution.

DOCUMENTATION

CHANGED: The documentation viewer now opens fixed English paths (docs/readme.txt, docs/game_guide.txt, docs/changes.txt) and lists only the files that actually exist.
CHANGED: This changelog was rewritten from scratch for version 1.0.0 and its sections were consolidated, so no topic appears twice.

CLEANUP

REMOVED: Dead code found in a full source sweep: the unused session clock string and its timer, the unused focused item variable, the readable time helper that only served the session clock, the include that loaded it, and the orphaned daily message and game time save files.
CHECKED: All .nvgt sources, map lines, settings files and includes were searched for broken, disabled, commented out, duplicated, fallback, generic, placeholder and stub code. The remaining candidate matches were legitimate API returns, real configuration defaults, encrypted and legacy file readers that still open real files, and asset resolution paths that must exist.

ENEMIES AND ALLIES

ADDED: Enemies and allies that live entirely offline, with no network, no player list and no server messages. They spawn from a spawner line stored on the map, patrol the whole map and keep respawning after the configured delay.
ADDED: An "enemy and ally spawner" entry in the construction menu that asks for the number of enemies, the number of allies and the respawn time in milliseconds, and writes them to the map.
ADDED: Both sides fight with the player's own weapon files. Fire interval, fire mode, range, damage, magazine capacity, reserve ammunition, reload sound, draw sound and the walk weight come from the .wpm file of each weapon, an NPC switches weapons when its own runs dry and it falls back to melee.
CHANGED: The walking pace of every NPC is random and slower than the player's. The weapon weight is added on top of that pace instead of replacing it, and melee weapons carry no weight penalty.
ADDED: Enemies target allies and the player; allies only target enemies. Nobody damages the player during an exchange between an enemy and an ally: the player is hurt only when an enemy has chosen the player as its target.
FIXED: An NPC no longer closes all the way to the target to line up a shot. Outside its weapon range it closes the distance, and once inside it sidesteps onto a firing line on the axis that leaves the greater distance, so a weapon is used at the range its own file declares.
ADDED: Enemies and allies carry their own random ammunition, use healing items with a 5 to 10 second cooldown and equip a shield 5 to 10 seconds after spawning.
ADDED: Kills are announced globally in English with attacker, victim, weapon and coordinates, for example "Enemy killed Friend at 500, 300, 0 with Mp5."
ADDED: The player's projectiles now hit enemies and allies.
ADDED: Enemies and allies pick items up from the ground. When a character has no ammunition left for any firearm it carries and no healing items at all, it searches for a useful item within 25 steps, walks to it and collects it automatically from three steps away, exactly like the player does. Ammunition goes into the matching weapon's reserve and healing items go into its own supply. When nothing useful is nearby it keeps patrolling as usual.
ADDED: The patrol and walking method is the one used by the original individual enemy and friend files: a target anywhere on the map is chosen, the character walks one tile per walking interval on both axes at once with the original facing values, and it only opens fire when the target is inside its own weapon's range, on one of the four cardinal lines and with a clear line of sight.
ADDED: A map that carries a safe zone disables combat for enemies and allies entirely. They keep patrolling and collecting items on that map, but they never target or attack anybody while they are there.

REVERB ZONES

ADDED: Reverb zones stored on maps and built with a form that offers only the native reverb of King Audio: decay, wet and dry level, diffusion, density, early and late delay, high frequency reference, high frequency decay ratio, low shelf frequency and gain, high cut and the early/late mix. No other effect is used.
ADDED: A list of platform sounds with loop playback inside the reverb form, so a zone can be tuned while hearing the surface the map uses.
ADDED: Every sound pool the player hears is routed through one reverb bus, so a zone reverberates everything and no zone leaves the game dry.
ADDED: The reverb zone builder now offers a reverb type list before the sliders: room, small room, long room, hall, cathedral, cave, bathroom, arena, hangar, forest, tunnel and underwater. Choosing a type sets every slider at once.
FIXED: The looping platform sound of the builder now goes through its own mixer with the native King Audio reverb attached, so the effect is heard while the sliders and the type are being changed instead of only after the zone is saved. Any slider change reconfigures the loop immediately.

MAPS

ADDED: Map lines for reverb zones and for the enemy and ally spawner.
REMOVED: The reward for discovering a map. Finding a new map no longer pays gold, plays an unlock sound or announces a reward; discovery is only bookkeeping.
FIXED: Saving the enemy and ally spawner rewrote the map from the parsed cache and could drop unrelated lines such as a map sound source. The spawner now reads the map file itself, replaces only its own line and keeps every other line untouched.
FIXED: The platform form lists exactly the folders that exist under "Map Platforms". Names from the deleted material registry no longer appear, which also ends the duplicates they created, such as the folder "Dirt" and the registry entry "dirt" listed side by side.
ADDED: Volume and pitch sliders in the platform form, the same pair the sound source and ambience builders use. Both act on the running preview, so the floor sound is judged by ear before the platform is added.
FIXED: Platform previews played 8 to 20 dB down because the shared preview sound stayed routed to the reverb builder's mixer after that form had been used. The preview now returns to the master bus and plays at the volume of the slider.

RESOURCES

REMOVED: The old ground resource system. The built-in materials are no longer defined or registered, the materials category is gone from the item editor, and an item that still carries that category in an old settings file is dropped when the item list is built.
REMOVED: The "Allow collectable resources from this platform" checkbox, the resource name field and the collectible field written into platform lines. Older maps that still carry that extra field load normally, with the field read as nothing.

ITEMS

ADDED: A tandem warhead rocket and a ray beam cell, two ammunition types that did not exist in the ammo set.

PLAYER

FIXED: Dying now empties the inventory. The death save wrote the inventory as it was, so the items came back with the next life; the inventory and the equipment are cleared before the death save is written.
FIXED: Firing is blocked inside a safe zone even when a firing path does not go through the game loop's own safe zone check.
ADDED: The player's death message now reports the coordinates and the weapon, for example "You were killed by an enemy at 300, 0, 0 with Mp5 on the safe map."
