Changelog - The Confront
========================

This document records the chronological history of updates, improvements, and fixes for The Confront by release date and time.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 21, 2026 at 11:00
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Audio-Backed Weapon Updates:
   - Sword: Configured new dedicated audio (draw, 3 fire swings, 3 impacts) with effective range of 7 steps.
   - Axe: Updated audio set (draw, 3 swings, 3 impacts) with effective range of 5 steps.
   - Knife: Added new dedicated melee knife configuration (draw, 3 swings, 3 impacts) with range of 3 steps.
   - Rifle: Added new rifle weapon configuration (draw, empty, 3 fire sounds, 3 impacts, reload) with range of 20 steps.
   - Machete: Permanently removed obsolete machete configuration and audio.

2. Release Package & On-Demand Sound Delivery:
   - Implemented automated missing sounds detection at startup: if the game directory lacks sounds, an accessible prompt offers to download and extract the sounds package (Y/Enter) or proceed without sounds (Escape).
   - Downloads run via the native updater engine with tone progress beeps and automatic zip extraction.
   - Standalone lightweight release bundle prepared for GitHub distribution without the ~2 GB audio payload.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 21, 2026 at 10:20
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. WPM-Driven Weapon Configuration:
   - Verified and enforced that all weapons load stats, ranges, damages, weights, fire rates, and audio exclusively from individual .wpm configuration files without hardcoded values in code.
   - Fire rates strictly adhere to a single exact firetime interval per weapon with zero min/max oscillation.

2. Reload Sound System & Fallback Removal:
   - Removed generic reload sound fallbacks across the ammo and weapon engines; weapons with missing reload sound entries reload silently via a dedicated 2000ms safety timer without generic sound substitution.
   - Audited the sound library and pruned weapons that lacked reload sounds or complete sound profiles (colt1911, magnum revouver, tauros revouver, tar 21 assalt rifle, beneli m4, ithica_m37, ks123), ensuring every remaining firearm is backed by verified audio.

3. Weapon Range and Damage Rebalancing:
   - Melee: Short ranges (4 to 7 steps) and balanced damage (knife: 180-280, sword: 260-400, axe: 350-500, etc.).
   - Pistols: Ranges between 9 and 15 steps; damages scaled between 480 and 790.
   - Revolvers: Ranges between 14 and 20 steps; damages scaled between 400 and 680.
   - Shotguns: Ranges between 20 and 32 steps; damages scaled between 550 and 1550.
   - Machine Guns & Submachine Guns: Ranges between 22 and 45 steps; damage rebalanced to high-tier 1000 to 4500, eliminating underpowered 50-150 damage values.
   - Rifles: Ranges between 35 and 50 steps; damages scaled between 610 and 1500.
   - Sniper Rifles: Long ranges between 52 and 65 steps; damages scaled between 900 and 3500.
   - Heavy Artillery & Launchers: Cannon range scaled to 70 steps (5000-6000 damage), crossbow to 30 steps, flamethrower to 10 steps.

4. Source Code and Sound Tree Cleanup:
   - Pruned orphaned sound directories and unreferenced weapon aliases.
   - Removed unused stub files (dlgplayer.nvgt), empty legacy stub functions (init_sound_package), dead language directories (sounds/boas-vindas), and unreferenced utilities in hip.nvgt.

5. Update System Audio:
   - Configured the new update discovery audio (updatFound.ogg) played when a new version is detected.
   - Preserved silent background download with native NVGT tone generator percentage progress beeps without background music playback.
   - Configured update completion audio playback (updatecomplete.ogg) upon successful package installation.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 20, 2026 at 20:25
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Long-Distance 4-Cardinal Weapon Hits:
   - Fixed long-range weapon hit detection so firearms (rifles, sniper rifles, pistols, revolvers, shotguns) hit aligned targets at any distance within weapon range in all 4 cardinal directions (straight ahead, straight behind, straight left, and straight right).
   - Removed sampled raycast bottlenecks that prematurely dropped long-range shots. Solid walls remain the only obstacles capable of blocking direct fire.
   - Expanded effective weapon ranges: rifles now reach 80 to 150 tiles, pistols 35 to 50 tiles, revolvers 45 to 60 tiles, and shotguns 25 to 35 tiles.
   - Expanded NPC positional sound distance to 250 tiles so 3D audio hit impacts, pain sounds, and death announcements are clearly heard across the map.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 20, 2026 at 19:45
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Weapon Targeting Across 4 Cardinal Directions:
   - Fixed weapon hit detection so targets aligned in all four cardinal lines (straight ahead, straight behind, straight left, and straight right) are valid and hit within range and line of sight.
   - Weapons automatically target the closest valid enemy among the 4 directions rather than ignoring targets to the left, right, or rear.
   - Non-cardinal (diagonal) targets remain strictly invalid and unhit.

2. Weapon Damage Rebalancing:
   - Pistols: Rebalanced damages with rich variations across all pistols, ranging between 480 and 790 damage.
   - Revolvers: Rebalanced damages ranging between 400 and 700 damage.
   - Rifles: Rebalanced damages ranging between 610 and 1500 damage.
   - Shotguns: Rebalanced damages ranging between 550 and 1550 damage.
   - Sniper Rifles: Rebalanced damages ranging between 900 and 3500 damage.

3. Player Maximum Health:
   - Raised player base maximum health from 1000 to 5000 to harmonize combat pacing and weapon power.

4. Language Consistency:
   - Standardized all in-game text and speech to English with zero language mixing.
   - Cleaned store welcome speech and NPC spawner dialogs into concise English.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 20, 2026 at 19:15
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. NPC AI and Map Performance:
   - Zero-allocation A* pathfinding using static generation-stamped buffers.
   - Smooth 60+ FPS without player movement stutter or RAM spikes regardless of NPC count.
   - Waypoint navigation guiding NPCs around walls and obstacles without straight-line beelining.
   - Dynamic 3D audio updates restricted to active voices to eliminate CPU overhead.

2. Unified Single-Line Map Spawners:
   - Map spawner format consolidated into a single line per multi-NPC group:
     Format: npcspawner:<id1,id2,...>:<count>:<respawn_ms>:<x0>:<y0>:<z0>:<x1>:<y1>:<z1>:<respawn_after_death>
   - Spawners strictly respect configured quantities above zero without creating duplicate instances.
   - Initial map entry delay: NPCs wait for the full configured millisecond timer before spawning randomly.
   - "Only respawn after death" configuration: dead NPCs only respawn if enabled, waiting the exact configured cooldown.

3. Player Death Announcement:
   - Removed spatial coordinates and map names from death broadcasts, announcing strictly who killed the player.
