Welcome to The Confront, an accessible 3D shooting game.

OBJECTIVE
Survive: fight enemies, avoid defeat, gain experience, and progress through levels and resurrections.

PROGRESSION AND MAPS
Maps can have level and resurrection requirements. Challenges and enemies appear as you progress. Maps opened for testing may be locked again in later versions.

STORES AND CURRENCIES
The game has two currencies: Gold and Golden Crystal. Enemies can reward these currencies and items. Stores are placed on maps with the builder. Press Shift+Enter inside a store, use the arrow keys to choose a category, and press Tab to browse its items. Each category sets prices and which of the two currencies it charges.

DEFAULT CONTROLS
Arrow keys: move. Q/E: turn. Space: jump. Left Ctrl: fire. R: reload. A: check ammunition. I: open the full inventory. Arrow keys: browse items. Home/End: first/last item. Tab/Shift+Tab: change category. Space: read the item description. Shift+1 through Shift+0: assign the focused inventory item as a favorite. 1 through 0: use the matching favorite. Shift+Enter: interact with a store at your location. Alt+Enter: enter an elevator, choose a floor, or exit; outside an elevator, use a valid TravelPoint if the destination allows entry. C: coordinates. Z: location. H: health. L: level and progress. K: kills. B: builder. P: pause. Esc: exit.

Controls can be changed in settings. The Confront is under active development; maps and rules can change between versions.
