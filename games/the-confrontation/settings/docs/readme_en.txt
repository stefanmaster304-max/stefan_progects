Welcome to The Confront, an accessible 3D shooting game.

OBJECTIVE
Survive: fight enemies, avoid defeat, gain experience, and progress through levels and resurrections. Battles can reward coins and items to improve your equipment.

PROGRESSION AND MAPS
Maps can have level and resurrection requirements. New challenges and enemies appear as you progress. Some maps may be opened for testing and locked again later; the game reports the requirements for entry.

STORES AND REWARDS
Enemies can reward experience, coins, and items. Stores are placed on maps with the builder. Enter a store's area and press Enter. Use the arrow keys to move between its categories and Tab to browse items in the selected category. Prices and accepted currencies follow that category's configuration.

DEFAULT CONTROLS
Arrow keys: move. Q/E: turn. Space: jump. Left Ctrl: fire. R: reload. A: check ammunition. Tab: browse inventory. Enter: use or open a store at its location. Alt+Enter: travel silently when standing in a valid TravelPoint and the destination allows entry. C: coordinates. Z: location. H: health. L: level and progress. K: kills. B: builder. P: pause. Esc: exit.

Controls can be changed in settings. The Confront is under active development; maps and rules can change between versions.
