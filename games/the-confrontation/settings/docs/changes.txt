Changelog - The Confront
========================

This document records the chronological history of updates, improvements, and fixes for The Confront by release date and time.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 22, 2026 at 19:10
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   - Moved bgt_compat.nvgt from the root directory into includes/bgt_compat.nvgt and updated project include in dw.nvgt.

   - Implemented character-aware store sound resolution via get_store_sound(): store welcome (st.ogg), purchase success (buy.ogg), and store error (store_error.ogg) audio now correctly load from the active character's audio folder (Stefan and Ilana).
   - Both characters now cleanly play their respective greetings and purchase confirmations in the store.

3. Store Purchases Inventory Persistence:
   - Implemented load_player_store_inventory() and save_player_store_inventory() in store_menu.nvgt.
   - When entering the store, the active character's save file is loaded to display and use their true currency balances.
   - Every store purchase immediately writes the updated inventory and deducted currency to the character's save file (gamesave_<character>.dat).
   - When entering any map, all purchased items are preserved in the player's inventory.
   - Guarded store access to require selecting a character first in Options.

     - Stefan (Masculine): Athletic, toned body with blond hair and long beard, holding a double-barreled machine gun. 5,000 base HP, moves slower than standard speed (28% increased movement interval), exclusive Tristan pain sounds (1-16) and death voice 17.

New on November 21, 2026 at 12:15

1. Weapon Firing Key & Timing Precision:
   - Enforced Left Control (KEY_LCONTROL) as the sole weapon firing key (Right Control never fires).
   - Resolved a critical firing loop timing bug: previously, holding down the trigger on semi-automatic weapons prematurely restarted the fire timer on every frame without firing, causing subsequent key taps to be dropped and creating the illusion of oscillating fire rates or key failures.

New on November 21, 2026 at 10:20

1. WPM-Driven Weapon Configuration:
   - Verified and enforced that all weapons load stats, ranges, damages, weights, fire rates, and audio exclusively from individual .wpm configuration files without hardcoded values in code.

   - Revolvers: Ranges between 14 and 20 steps; damages scaled between 400 and 680.

   - Machine Guns & Submachine Guns: Ranges between 22 and 45 steps; damage rebalanced to high-tier 1000 to 4500, eliminating underpowered 50-150 damage values.
   - Rifles: Ranges between 20 and 50 steps; damages scaled between 610 and 1500.
   - Sniper Rifles: Long ranges between 50 and 65 steps; damages scaled between 900 and 3500.
   - Heavy Artillery & Launchers: Cannon range scaled to 70 steps (5000-6000 damage), crossbow to 30 steps, flamethrower to 10 steps.

   - Pruned orphaned sound directories and unreferenced weapon aliases.
