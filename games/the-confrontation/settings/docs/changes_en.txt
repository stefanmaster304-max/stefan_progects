The Confront - Changelog (English)

New in version 1.3 (2026-11-28 - 11:03 -03:00)

MiniAudio Sound Engine, Steam Audio HRTF & Doors:
- Returned to NVGT's default MiniAudio sound engine (0.90.0-dev) for better fluidity, using Steam Audio (`nvgt_phonon`) for 3D HRTF spatialization and attenuation (`audio_panner_phonon_hrtf` and `audio_attenuator_phonon`), along with native `audio_fdn_reverb_node` and `reverb3d` for reverb zones (`includes/reverb_system.nvgt`, `includes/sound_pool.nvgt`, and `tcf.nvgt`).
- Completely removed the interior/exterior room acoustic filter from doors (`includes/door_system.nvgt`, `includes/builder/misc/door_builder.nvgt`, `includes/world_runtime.nvgt`, `includes/sound_pool.nvgt`, and `settings/maps/safe_zone.map`), while keeping 3D spatial door sounds at the door's exact coordinates.

New in version 1.2 (2026-11-27 - 10:55 -03:00)

Explosives System, Guided Missile & Fixes:
- Added a full explosives system (`includes/explosive_data_saver.nvgt`, `includes/explosive_manager.nvgt`, and `includes/explosive_system.nvgt`) using `.config` files in `settings/explosives/` bundled into `contents.dat` and managed via the development menu (`Explosive Manager`), dynamically hiding fields that do not apply to the selected explosive type.
- Added support for multiple explosive types: remote C4, sticky Semtex, dedicated remote detonator item (`remote_detonator`, detonating one charge at a time without being consumed), player-programmable timed bombs, frag grenades, smoke grenades, smoke bombs, landmines, and guided missiles.
- Added global item use/equip cooldown (`450 ms` in `includes/inventory.nvgt`) to prevent rapid scripted use, and removed verbose speech announcements when planting or throwing explosives.
- Nearby explosions now also damage the player's equipped shield (`shield_process_damage`) in addition to splash damage, and explosives cannot be armed or detonated inside safe zones.
- Landmines: ignore the player alone and detonate when an NPC enters the trigger radius, damaging and enraging NPCs (and damaging the player if standing within the blast radius).
- Smoke grenade vs. Smoke bomb: smoke grenades blind and stun NPCs of any class for 5 seconds (configurable), whereas smoke bombs keep NPCs within the radius stunned for their entire active duration; players are unaffected by smoke, while taking attenuated blast damage if standing inside damaging explosions.
- Full 3D explosive audio: added specific impact hit sound (`sound_hit`), louder blast playback, and distant explosion sound (`sound_dist`) with map-span attenuation so it propagates across the entire map upon detonation.
- Guided missiles: allow choosing between entering manual coordinates or selecting an active NPC target from an `audio_form` list, flying with 3D positional audio at the configured travel speed and detonating on impact.
- Owner aggro tracking: when any explosive or missile damages an NPC—even from afar—the NPC identifies the owner and immediately starts running toward the target.
- Kept only `sound_pickup` in items (`includes/item_data_saver.nvgt` and `includes/item_manager.nvgt`), removing unused `sound_draw`, `sound_plant`, and `sound_loop` fields.
- Fixed dynamic map loading in `get_map_content_by_name()` (`includes/map.nvgt`) and `save_to_map()` synchronization (`includes/builder.nvgt`) so resurrection/level restrictions take effect immediately on travel points for all maps.

New in version 1.1 (2026-11-27 - 09:40 -03:00)

Combat, Tracking, Audio & Localization:
- Fixed NPC impact sound playback in `npc_attack_player()` (`includes/npc_system.nvgt`): while the player (Stefan or Annie) has a shield equipped, the NPC's player-hit sound is suppressed so only the NPC's attack sound and the shield's own impact sound play; the NPC hit sound plays normally when unshielded.
- Added the `W` hotkey (`track_travel_status` in `includes/keybinds.nvgt` and `includes/tracking_system.nvgt`) to query the real-time distance and direction of the currently tracked travel point.
- Added bidirectional room acoustic filtering on doors (`includes/door_system.nvgt` and `includes/sound_pool.nvgt`), muffling interior sounds from outside while the door is closed and restoring full clarity when opened.
- Localized the `"Your health is already at maximum."` message (`inventory.health_already_full` in `includes/inventory.nvgt` and EN/PT/ES language files).

New in version 1.0 (2026-11-26 - 19:08 -03:00)

Official 1.0 Release & New NPC Class:
- Official `1.0` release (removed public beta / in-development status).
- Map `reverb:` lines now save and parse named `key=value` parameters (`x1=...:preset=...:decay_ms=...:wet_db=...:affect_sources=...`) for clear readability and editing in the map editor (with backward compatibility for positional lines).
- Added the "Affect sound sources and ambient sounds" checkbox to the Reverb Zone Builder (`reverb_builder.nvgt` and `reverb_system.nvgt`), keeping 3D sound sources and ambient sounds (`sp`) dry when unchecked.
- Added the new `boss` NPC class (`Boss` in English, `Chefe` in Portuguese, `Jefe` in Spanish) in `includes/npc_data.nvgt`, `includes/npc_manager.nvgt`, and language files.
- Updated the official website with `1.0` downloads without sounds, with full sounds, and link to the GitHub source code.

New in version 0.1.6 (2026-11-26 - 16:23 -03:00)

Updater, NPC Tracking, Contents & Sound Pack:
- Game updater (`apply_game_update` in `includes/updater.nvgt`) now downloads only the `tcf.exe` executable directly from the GitHub release, validates its integrity (PE `MZ` header and minimum size), and replaces it safely with backup/rollback instead of downloading a full `.zip`.
- NPC tracking (`track_nearby_npcs` in `includes/tracking_system.nvgt` and EN/PT/ES translations) now announces the NPC's current health (`npc.hp`) along with real-time distance and direction.
- Encrypted and updated `settings/contents.dat` and `settings/maps.dat` bundles with all newly added NPCs and recent map updates.
- Fixed the internal version string (`ver = "0.1.6"`) in `tcf.nvgt` so the compiled executable properly displays and saves version `0.1.6` after updating.
- Added automatic spoken download progress announcements every 10% in `dl_file` (`includes/updater.nvgt`), while keeping `Spacebar` progress checks active.
- Removed all updater progress and notification beeps (`beep_percentage`, `updater_beep`, and mute key `M` in `includes/updater.nvgt`).
- Added continuous looping playback of `sounds/updater/update_music.ogg` while any update (game, contents, maps, or sounds) is in progress, stopping cleanly before swapping directories or upon completion/cancellation.
- Updated the sound package (`0.1.6`) to follow the game version numbering, replacing the previous sound pack for both updates and automatic initial download when sounds are missing.

New in version 0.1.5 (2026-11-26 - 10:15 -03:00)

Weapon System & Alignment:
- Updated weapon target acquisition (`get_weapon_target_in_range` in `includes/npc_system.nvgt`) to require exact cardinal alignment with the player (`npc.x == px` or `npc.y == py`).
- Weapons now hit NPCs within their configured WPM range (with unobstructed line of sight) only when the target is aligned straight ahead, straight behind, straight left, or straight right.

Compiled Bundles, Inventory, Audio & Updater:
- Rebuilt and updated the encrypted `settings/maps.dat` and `settings/contents.dat` bundles from the latest files in `settings/maps/`, `settings/npcs/`, items, weapons, stores, and packages, fixing the issue where recently edited maps (such as Lion's Cave, with native reverb zones and updated platforms) did not reflect in the compiled executable (`tcf.exe`).

New in version 0.1.3 (2026-11-26 - 09:51 -03:00)

Inventory Fixes:
- Fixed the total item count announced for inventory categories (`speak_inventory_category`):
  * Previously, the inventory summed up the stack quantities of all items (e.g., 55 potions + 55 shields were announced as 110 items total).
  * Now, the total count reflects only the unique item types in the category (e.g., 50 potions = 1 item total; revolver + sword + shield + knife = 4 items total), while individual stack quantities continue to be spoken when focusing each item.

New in version 0.1.2 (2026-11-26 - 09:29 -03:00)

Native King Audio Engine Reverberation System:
- Added a full reverberation zone system using strictly King Audio Engine's native mixer reverb (no high-pass, no low-pass, no extra DSP effects, and no MiniAudio fallback).
- Fixed a null pointer access crash in `init_world_reverb_mixer()` at startup before `npcp` is instantiated, routing the NPC pool to the reverb mixer once the map loads.
- Translated the entire Reverb Zone Builder menu (`reverb zone`) into English, Portuguese, and Spanish via `tr()`.
- Added XYZ-bounded reverb zones (`minx`, `maxx`, `miny`, `maxy`, `minz`, `maxz`) saved and loaded in map files (`reverb:...`), automatically applying and clearing reverb in real time as the player enters or leaves a zone.
- Added a Reverb Zone Builder (`reverb zone`) in the builder menu featuring:
  * A list with only valid King Audio presets (`room`, `hall`, `cave`, `bathroom`, `arena`, `hangar`, `forest`, `tunnel`, `underwater`, and `custom`).
  * A list of available platform sounds played in a continuous loop while configuring the zone.
  * Arrow-key sliders for all native King Audio reverb parameters (`Wet level`, `Dry level`, `Decay time`, `Diffusion`, `Density`, `Early delay`, `Late delay`, `HF reference`, `HF decay ratio`, `Low shelf freq`, `Low shelf gain`, `High cut`, and `Early/Late mix`) with live real-time audio updates.

New in version 0.1.1 (2026-11-25 - 19:02 -03:00)

Gameplay, Physics & Audio Fixes:
- Complete jump fix (Stefan and Annie):
  * Fixed the issue where the character only moved 1 coordinate up on the Z axis and remained stuck walking in mid-air when jumping while running.
  * Removed the premature checkforplatforms() call during the ascending phase, which was cancelling the jump 1 unit above platforms with Z thickness or staircases.
  * Removed premature maxz clamping during jumping and mid-air horizontal movement, ensuring the character properly ascends from 0 up to 5 (jumpheight = 5), holds at the apex, and descends smoothly to the ground or platform.
  * Structured the jump state machine to prevent same-frame conflicts and state leakage across consecutive jumps or elevators.
- Unknown area announcement on zoneless maps:
  * Fixed the location system so that when a map has no zones defined, the game properly announces "Unknown area" on map load and when checking location (Z key).
- Free-fall fix:
  * Properly reset the fall timer and fall counter when entering free-fall outside a jump or stepping off a platform.
- Jump keybind, inventory & staircases:
  * Jumping now honors the remappable jump keybind in addition to Spacebar.
  * Fixed inventory open (invopen.ogg), close (invclose.ogg), and navigation (invmove.ogg to invmove5.ogg) sound paths.
  * Fixed staircase jump checking and localized the sitting hint when attempting to climb stairs while seated.
- Dead-code cleanup:
  * Removed unreferenced functions and variables across modules and deduplicated integrity checks.
