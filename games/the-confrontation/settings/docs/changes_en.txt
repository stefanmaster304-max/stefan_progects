Changelog - The Confront
========================

This document records the chronological history of updates, improvements, and fixes for The Confront by release date and time.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 23, 2026 at 19:40 (v0.5 Updater with Music & Automatic ZIP Download)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Background Music During Updater Progress:
   - While downloading and extracting any update (sounds, game executable, contents, and maps), the updater now plays the updater soundtrack (updater mus.mp3) simultaneously with the rising progress beeps. Both audio layers play together without replacing or muting each other.

2. Automatic Full ZIP Package Download:
   - Removed the prompt asking whether to download only the executable or the zip file. The updater now automatically downloads the full ZIP package (the_confront_v0.5.zip), extracts all updated binaries, translations, configurations, and changelogs, and restarts automatically.

3. Refined Sit and Stand Sound Effects:
   - The platform fall sound (fall) plays specifically upon sitting down. Standing up now plays only the character stand sound (stand).

4. Player Saves and Settings Relocated to AppData:
   - Player game saves (gamesave_*.dat) and settings.dat are now stored in AppData/Roaming/Stefan Projects/The Confront/, leaving the game's settings folder strictly for encrypted game assets.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 23, 2026 at 18:15 (v0.5 Audio & Annie Update)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Platform Fall Sound on Sitting and Standing:
   - When pressing S to sit down or stand up, the game now plays the exact platform fall sound (fall.ogg) together with sit.ogg or stand.ogg.

2. Female Character Renamed to Annie:
   - The female character has been renamed from Ilana to Annie, fully mapped to her audio assets (sounds/players/hane/) including Aria voice lines, with automatic save migration.

3. Soundtrack Folder and Updated Sound Package:
   - Music directory standardized to sounds/Music/, and a brand new sounds.zip package has been packaged and published.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 23, 2026 at 12:25 (v0.5 Update)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Starter Pack Audio Fix in Compiled Mode:
   - Fixed package opening sound in the compiled client with robust fallback to pack.ogg and _packageopen.ogg and reliable package registration.

2. Platform Footstep Sound on Sitting and Standing:
   - Pressing the S key to sit down now plays the sound of the platform currently stepped on (grass, concrete, wood, stone, dirt, etc.) together with the sit sound.
   - Standing up also plays the platform step sound harmoniously alongside the stand sound.

3. Complete Deletion of Temporary Update Folders:
   - The update_temp folder and all downloaded payloads (contents_payload, maps_payload, scripts, archives) are now completely and immediately removed before client restart and upon startup.

4. NVDA-Style Progress Beeps Across All Downloads:
   - The pure sine-wave rising pitch progress bar beeping system is now active across all game downloads: sound packs, content, maps, and executable updates.

5. Full Updater Localization:
   - Localized download help messages and keyboard queries (Space for percentage, 1/2/3 for detailed stats, M for mute/unmute) across Portuguese, English, and Spanish.

6. Official Website Redesign:
   - New welcoming home page for Stefan Projects with clear navigation to projects.
   - Projects page prominently highlighting The Confront, gameplay mechanics, and test maps.
   - The Confront official page restructured: comprehensive game description and features placed first, and download options positioned at the bottom.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 23, 2026 at 11:45 (v0.4 Build 4 Update)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Map Compilation & Bears Valley Hidden:
   - "Bears Valey" map was encrypted into settings/maps.dat with the minimum 4th resurrection requirement, ensuring it stays hidden from early access in compiled clients.
   - Added and compiled the new "Robots World" (robots map) featuring advanced robot spawners, concrete platform, and dynamic city audio ambiance.

2. New NPCs Added & Encrypted into Content Bundle:
   - Mine Robocop (mine_robocop.npc): tactical armored robot wielding a metal shield, 5,500 HP, custom footstep audio, and precision shots.
   - Patrol (patrol.npc): agile scout patrol robot with 5,400 HP, rapid fire, and mechanical 3D voice vocalizations.
   - Both new NPCs were encrypted into settings/contents.dat with balanced coin drops (gold, silver, crystals, and diamonds).

3. Content and Map Updates Deployed:
   - Fresh maps.dat and contents.dat packages deployed to update channels.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 23, 2026 at 11:10 (v0.4 Build 3 Update)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Complete Trilingual Documentation & Expanded Help Menu:
   - Readme and Changelog documents are now provided in all three supported languages: English, Portuguese, and Spanish.
   - The in-game documentation menu now provides dedicated choices to browse recent changes and readmes in English, Portuguese, and Spanish.

2. Virtual Accessible Dialog for Sound Package Download:
   - Replaced any standard Windows dialog boxes with native virtual audio dialogs in NVGT.
   - Fully accessible with Y or Enter to confirm download, and Escape or N to cancel and continue playing without sound.

3. Spoken Extraction Notification & NVDA-Style Progress Beeps:
   - When extracting downloaded sound packages, the game announces extraction progress verbally and plays rising-pitch tone beeps similar to NVDA progress bars, followed by a completion confirmation cue.

4. Clean Temporary Directory Teardown:
   - Any temporary update folder and intermediate files (such as update_temp) are completely wiped from the disk after successful installation of contents, maps, sounds, or game executables.

5. Single-Grant Starter Pack Rule:
   - The Starter Pack is granted only once upon initial character creation. Re-entering maps or switching levels no longer grants duplicate starter packs.

6. Refined Death Consequences:
   - The player's carried inventory is preserved upon death, but currently equipped weapons are automatically unequipped and worn shields are removed.

7. Bulletproof Executable Replacement:
   - Updater replacement scripts now wait for process exit and perform safe file renames to prevent Windows file-lock errors during auto-updates.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New on November 23, 2026 at 10:20 (v0.4 Build 2 Update)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Updater Download Choice Dialog:
   - When updating the game, an accessible dialog prompts: "What do you want to download?" with options:
     * "Only the executable": downloads and replaces tcf.exe directly.
     * "Full ZIP archive": downloads the complete release archive with changelogs, configs, and binaries.

2. Sit / Stand Keybind and Sound Fix:
   - Key S reliably toggles sitting and standing.
   - Plays designated character-specific sitting (sit.ogg) and standing (stand.ogg) audio cues.

3. Reduced Unnecessary Spoken Chatter:
   - Silenced repetitive voice announcements when drinking potions, consuming anti-death cards, equipping weapons, and breaking shields (sound effects remain active).

4. NPC Currency Drops Rebalanced:
   - Configured balanced coin loot on defeat: Rabbit drops 50 golden crystals + random silver/gold/diamond; Coyote drops 100 diamonds + currencies; Wolf drops 150 gold + currencies and anti-death card; Bear, Dog, and Robocop drop balanced low random coins.
